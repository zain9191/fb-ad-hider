AI detection

Facebook Ad Hider can ask TypeSafe AI (model Jev) about feed posts that its local rules can't classify.

Privacy

When AI detection is on, the text of posts that local rules can't classify is sent to TypeSafe AI (api.typesafe.ai) for classification.

Only posts that stay on your screen for about a second are sent (not posts below it), normally once per browser session; a post can be sent again after a temporary error such as a rate limit. What is sent: the post's message and link titles (up to 1,500 characters), the author's name, up to 5 outbound link hostnames (no full URLs), whether an ad-style button is shown, and the page language. Comments, reactions, the names of people who reacted, counters, the timestamp and your Facebook cookies are left out. Posts that the local rules recognise as ads or as "Suggested for you" are never sent.

Get a key at console.typesafe.ai/keys. The key is stored only in this browser (chrome.storage.local, not synced) and is used only by the extension's background worker.

Detection

Higher = fewer false positives (organic posts hidden by mistake), lower = more ads caught. Default 0.85.

When the cap is reached, the extension stops calling TypeSafe until midnight (local time); local rules keep working. TypeSafe bills per input token: see docs.typesafe.ai/models for current pricing.
